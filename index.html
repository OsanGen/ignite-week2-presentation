    COST RECORDS

    What Makes a Cost Record Traceable

    Part 1 of 3

    A traceable record lets someone connect a charge to the course, the run, and the activity or usage that caused it. It also makes the quantity, unit, rate, currency, period, and payer visible, with a source for the calculation.

    TRACEABILITY QUESTIONS

    • Which course and run?
    • Which activity or usage?
    • What quantity and unit?
    • Which rate and currency?
    • Which period and payer?
    • What source supports it?

    Part 2 of 3

    For example, P01 is the whole-run text-input line. It uses three hundred thousand tokens at a fictional rate of two dollars per million tokens. The billing quantity matters: multiplying by two without dividing by one million would apply the rate to the wrong unit.

    • Course | SYN-COURSE-001
    • Run | SYN-RUN-001
    • Usage | 300,000 tokens
    • Rate | $2.00 per 1,000,000 tokens
    • Currency | USD
    • Expected cost | $0.60
